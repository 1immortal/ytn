# YTN 1.12.4

## Watch: alerts, stats and power

Thanks again for the feedback in #12!

- **The alert list is no longer empty.** Tapping *Alerts (2)* on the watch showed "2 alerts" with nothing below it when phone alerts were off or hadn't run yet. Every refresh now sends the alert titles with the count, so they always match. (Titles still follow your privacy settings. If they're hidden, the watch says so.)
- **CPU, RAM and temperature** on the watch screen and the tile, for example *CPU 3% · RAM 41% · 38 °C*. These are only fetched while a watch is connected.
- **Emergency reboot and shut down from the watch.** Open *Power* on the watch, pick *Reboot* or *Shut down* and confirm. Your phone then shows a notification, and nothing happens until you confirm it on the phone. Turn it off any time in System › Phone alerts › *Reboot and shut down from the watch*.

Install 1.12.4 on **both** the phone and the watch to get everything. The two keep working together if only one is updated; you just won't see the new parts.

Works with TrueNAS 25.10 and 27 (27 is still a release candidate and hasn't been tested on a real system yet).

## Downloads

- `truenas-companion-release.apk`: phone/tablet app; installs over the existing release.
- `truenas-companion-debug.apk`: separate YTN Preview phone app.
- `truenas-companion-wear-release.apk`: Wear OS companion; update separately with `adb install -r`.

Each APK has a matching `.sha256` file. Existing signing identities are preserved.
